CastChat Black List

Users who end up on this list lose the right to offer suggestions or report issues; these privileges are revoked for those who break community rules and, as a result, find themselves on this list.

1 - If an individual on this list is insulted by another user, any report they file—whether on our platform or on our server—will be ignored.

2 - Users with an ❌ next to their name have committed a very serious offense; those with a ⚠️ next to their name have committed a less serious offense; and those with a ✅ next to their name, followed by a date, have a penalty period that will end on that date.

3 - Depending on the severity of the issue caused by the user, they could even lose their verification emoji.

4 - Reports concerning individuals on this list will only be accepted if another user witnesses the incident.
